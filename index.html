about

Nicholas D’Angelo is an artist and photographer whose work explores action, movement, the body, and psychological states. Working across photography, performance, and digital processes, he creates ethereal images that exist between physical presence and abstraction.

D’Angelo earned a B.A. in Studio Art from Montclair State University and an M.F.A. in Painting and 2D Design from Cranbrook Academy of Art. His work has been exhibited nationally and internationally, including at Sarah Spurgeon Gallery, Soil Art Gallery, T Mari Gallery, IU Kokomo Art Gallery, and Glasgow Gallery of Photography.

Artist Statement

My work explores action, movement, and emotional states through ethereal imagery and blurred motion, creating two-dimensional narratives that exist between perception and abstraction. Using my own body, gestures, and experiences as source material, I construct images that convey both physical energy and psychological tension. The resulting works suggest instability, transformation, and the fluid nature of memory and emotion, often occupying a space between recognition and ambiguity.

I am interested in how emotions can be difficult to define or interpret. Emotional experiences are layered, shifting, and influenced by memory, environment, and perception. Through soft focus, distortion, and motion, my work examines these unstable states and reflects the disorientation that can emerge from the speed and intensity of contemporary life. The blurred image becomes both a visual language and a metaphor for uncertainty, vulnerability, and the fragmentation of experience.
